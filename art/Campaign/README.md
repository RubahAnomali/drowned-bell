# Four-chapter art review

Reviewed the campaign production list, actor and prop atlases, pixel-mapped sprites, environment drawings, chapter bookends and Tidal Engine art pack. All additions in this folder are standalone pixel artwork.

| Chapter | Existing designs retained | Additions |
|---|---|---|
| I · Drowned Belfry | Bell portrait, diver, crab, jelly, Undertoll, route landmarks, Brass Lung and helmet HUD | Belfry lamp seal, saved notice and Continue design |
| II · Sluice Works | Limpet and Porter attack poses, both Warden tells, relief vents, key and hatch | Cutaway pump portrait, gauge record icon and inspection drawing, impeller, counterweight, closed/open bypass drain, recoil cels and save artwork |
| III · Sounding Archive | Ledger icon and detail, actor sheets and decoy, chapter plate, fixtures, key and order | Engraved Map Loft pipe diagram, recoil cels and save artwork |
| IV · Tidal Engine | Seven room plates, attack sets, third record, dawn nave and credits | Isolated dawn sky, separate bridge hazard sources, dressed Condenser plate, conditional memorial, completion layout and save artwork |

The chapter portraits are 192 × 288 px. The new Sluice portrait uses 11 colors. Continue, completion, dawn and Condenser compositions are 512 × 288 px. The gauge record has a 16 px icon and 64 px inspection illustration. The pipe diagram is 80 × 56 px.

Read the six 48 px Machinery cells from left to right: impeller, counterweight, closed drain, open drain, condenser vent, wave outlet. Recoil cells are 64 px, ordered Limpet, Porter, Moth and Widow. Seals run horizontally in chapter order, saved notices vertically.

Memorial cells measure 196 × 122 px. Read the four columns across each of two rows. Cell index is the sum of 1 for the gauge, 2 for the ledger, and 4 for the punched record. The names Mara Vale, Ivo Reed and Nel Orr are original lettering choices for this art pass. The final screen uses blank run-stat fields for layout. The overview combines the three existing chapter portraits with the new Sluice portrait. All display enlargements use nearest-neighbor.

Verification included opening all 18 PNGs, inspecting the portrait and keeper galleries and completion screen, checking the independence of all eight memorial combinations, and round-tripping the Sluice portrait through Aseprite pixel-for-pixel.
