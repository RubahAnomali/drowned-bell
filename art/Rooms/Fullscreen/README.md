# Fullscreen room and attack studies

These are staged art composites for a 1920 × 1080 display. The game's 512 × 288 composition fills that display at 3.75× using nearest-neighbor sampling. Open a still at 100% or play the [fullscreen attack reel](Attack%20poses.mp4). The four chapter GIFs preserve the original pixel grid. The MP4 is a compressed viewing copy.

The 31 stills cover all 27 campaign rooms and the movement lab, including both ends of the gallery and lab and both halves of the shaft. Actors use the project's sprites or pixel drawings. Enemies are staged at encounter markers, the diver stands on a mapped surface, and the five helmet icons show the upper-left HUD footprint. The twins in Recording Choir are staged together to test the clapper. Camera motion, gates changing state, lighting effects, temporary banners, damage lanes and combat timing need live captures for final sign-off.
