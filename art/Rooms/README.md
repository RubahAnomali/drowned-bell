# Drowned Bell room art

The campaign has 27 named rooms. The three new chapter folders contain an editable Aseprite document and a matching opaque PNG for each of the first 20. The seven Tidal Engine originals remain in [TidalEngine](../TidalEngine). The additional [Test Room](Test/Test%20Room.png) is the developer movement lab.

One tile spans 16 pixels. Align the top-left pixel of a new PNG with the room's top-left map corner. Standard plates measure 512 × 288 px. Gutter Shaft measures 512 × 576 px. Choir Gallery and the Test Room measure 1024 × 288 px. Preserve these dimensions for scrolling rooms.

The Aseprite layers run from the rear wall through the room landmark and surface details to fixed terrain. Exposed rock and one-way platforms have a bright upper edge. The static art leaves actors, pickups, moving hazards and state-changing doors to their separate sprites. Existing fixtures that duplicate a painted landmark, such as the Impeller Well rotor, can be compared with the landmark layer hidden during integration.

For Chapter IV the plates in route order are Dock, Cradle, Condenser, Watch, Stair, Gate and Nave. The [dressed Condenser plate](../Campaign/IV-Condenser-hazards.png) includes the two hazard sources. [WatchCollected](../TidalEngine/WatchCollected.png) supplies the empty Night Watch shelf.
