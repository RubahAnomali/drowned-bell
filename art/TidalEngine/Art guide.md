# Chapter IV · Tidal Engine

The seven room plates run from the dock lamp to the keeper memorial. The working engine uses slate, patinated iron and worn copper. Brass marks machinery the diver can operate. The sky warms to apricot in the drained nave. The cutter and breakwater beacon give the sunrise a human scale.

## Room and ending art

Dock, Cradle, Condenser, Watch, Stair, Gate and Nave are 512 × 288 px scene compositions. Credits provides an unlettered tablet for alternate text. CreditsLettered is the finished English composition. ChapterPlate is a 192 × 288 px chapter illustration.

## Actor art

Open Ballast Knight or Tidekeeper in Aseprite to inspect named animation tags and frame timings. Both face left. All cels are 128 × 96 px with generous transparent space for the weapon. The resting soles sit at y = 85. Each attack has twelve cels. The final anticipation pose holds for 400 ms.

| Attack | What to read before contact |
|---|---|
| Knight hammer | The head clears the helmet. Light shows through the gap under the handle and a bead of oil falls from the hinge. |
| Tidekeeper hammer | The weapon lifts above the crown and tips back before crossing the body. |
| Floor wave | Both hands draw toward the centerline. The hammer drops vertically and a crest travels along the floor. |
| Pressure lane | The nozzle projects horizontally at chest height before a narrow jet extends from it. |
| Echo | The forked weapon winds behind the shoulder. Two pale arcs follow the cross-body stroke. |

Attacks is a 12-column atlas in the order above. OpenCrown contains alternate hammer and floor-wave rows with the additional phase-two vane. ActorSupport contains idle, step and hurt cels. Discharge contains four frames each of the crest, jet and echo, in 128 × 40 px cells.
